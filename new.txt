PHIEN 9: TIM RA NGUYEN NHAN CRASH THAT + XOA 2 LOI HOI QUY
===============================================================================
- Crash "Tcl_AsyncDelete: async handler deleted by the wrong thread" (app
  tat ngay sau khi xuat xong) DA TIM RA GOC R:
  * Splash khoi dong chay Tk tren 1 thread daemon (de hien som truoc khi
    import PySide6 nang).
  * _play_finish() goi root.quit() BEN TRONG after-callback cua Tk.
  * Tk_Quit() goi tu timer callback tren thread KHONG phai main lam Tcl mat
    rang async handler -> khi process tat, Tk in loi nay roi abort (0x80000003).
  * Da co bien the: chi duong co finish() la hong; duong atexit (abort) thi
    an toan -> chung to loi nam dung choi).
- Cach sua (giu nguyen hien thi som):
  1. Bo root.quit() khoi _play_finish; chi return. Pump het len reschedule
     nen Tk mainloop tu thoat binh thuong, roi finally cua thread splash
     moi destroy root tren DUNG thread do.
  2. Them _abort_and_join() + atexit: neu app ket thuc khong di qua finish(),
     yeu thread splash dung vong lap va destroy root truoc khi interpreter tat.
  3. Bo fallback tkinter Tk() trong main() cua app.py: tao them Tk root o
     main thread khi thread splash con song cung gay loi nay.
- LUA CHON THU NGHIEM (de khong doan): Tk o thread phu + quit() trong
  after-callback = hong; bo quit(), chi de mainloop tu thoat = sach.
  Cai dat khac (defer root.update(), bo progressbar, bo logo) deu vo hai.
- AI: nhanh "server dang khoi dong" bi thieu o nhanh retry cua chat (chi
  register path co bao). Da bo sung -> het 4s im lang khi server thuc day.
